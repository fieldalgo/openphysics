# Changed

## English

- Studio: **model cards no longer have their own restore buttons.** The Optical waveguide and Heated silicon waveguide cards repeated half of the Library's newest offer (*Restore from this browser · … (restored separately)* with **Start new (no runs)**). A card whose model has a kept record not in the tab now says so in a line that is not a control, *Kept in this browser · 4 runs · Restore at the top of the page*; the card itself still opens the model. The switch note names the real route, *Library › Continue where you left off › Restore*. [Workspace](docs/packages/simcase/workspace.md#first-optical-study).
