# Changed

## English

- Studio: **the optical scope note names both boundaries.** Beside the Open boundary choice it read *PEC outer edges* for a closed window; it now reads *3 × 2.4 µm window, closed (PEC edges) or with an open boundary (PML 1 µm outside the open edges, PEC on the closed ones)* whichever is chosen. [Workspace](docs/packages/simcase/workspace.md).
