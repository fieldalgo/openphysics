# Added

## English

- Transient heat and voltage-driven Joule heating on 3D solids support finite thermal contact between matching split-node surfaces. Both return interval-end contact heat rates for energy accounting during heating and cooling. [Usage](docs/packages/galerkin/transient_heat3d.md).
