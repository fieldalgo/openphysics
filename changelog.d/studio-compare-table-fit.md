# Changed

## English

- Studio: **the Compare table fits at 1440 × 900.** Each Polarization cell has two lines (*TE-like*, then *98.2 % TE*); cells have 6 px side padding; the *Off · turn on in Study or Optical modes* hint moves from the n_g header to the line above the table when neither run computed n_g. The 14 columns, their order, the values at full precision and the `data-*` attributes are unchanged. At 1440 × 900 every column is visible with 42 to 44 px to spare (Linux, the 2-mode and 8-mode tables). On a phone the table still scrolls inside its box with Modes frozen, now with a right-edge shadow and the line *Scroll sideways for all 14 columns ›*. [Workspace](docs/packages/simcase/workspace.md).
