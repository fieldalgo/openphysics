# Added

## English

- `galerkin`: Steady 3D DC conduction on tagged tetrahedral meshes with voltage terminals, insulation, isotropic region conductivities, terminal currents, and element-wise Joule heat for thermal solves. [Contract and validation](docs/packages/galerkin/electric_steady3d.md).
