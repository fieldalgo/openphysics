# Changed

## English

- `simcase`: **Studio 3D checks W5 for cylinders and spheres (experimental).** For each stem the check raises for builder shapes, the server and the workspace show the engine's words as written, with the code, the tree mark and Mesh and Run's reasons. The last good shape stays greyed. There are 8 stems: a cylinder that touches or crosses a side or is not through the slab, a sphere that touches a side, spheres that touch or cross, a sphere beside a box, a feature below the mesher tolerance, and `circle_segments` on a cylinder. All 8 pass.

  [3D models](docs/packages/simcase/workspace.md#3d-models-experimental).

  [3D 모델](packages/simcase/workspace.md#3d-모델실험적).
