# Changed

## English

- Studio: **one closed-window search choice.** In a closed window, Physics › Optical modes now has **Modes to return**: (•) **Highest n_eff above 1.45** ( ) **Nearest to n_eff** with the field **n_eff to search near** (2.460 for silicon), in place of the check box *Search near n_eff* and its separate field, which stayed disabled until the box was ticked. The field is always enabled, and typing an index in it selects Nearest to n_eff. Highest is the box off and Nearest the box on: the document, the capabilities and the session are unchanged. [Workspace](docs/packages/simcase/workspace.md).

## 한국어

- Studio: **닫힌 창 탐색 선택이 하나가 되었습니다.** 닫힌 창에서 Physics › Optical modes에는 체크 상자 *Search near n_eff*와, 상자를 켜기 전에는 쓸 수 없던 별도 필드 대신 **Modes to return**이 있습니다: (•) **Highest n_eff above 1.45** ( ) **Nearest to n_eff**와 필드 **n_eff to search near**(실리콘이면 2.460). 필드는 늘 쓸 수 있고, 굴절률을 입력하면 Nearest to n_eff가 선택됩니다. Highest는 상자를 끈 것, Nearest는 켠 것이므로 문서, capabilities, 세션은 바뀌지 않습니다. [워크스페이스](packages/simcase/workspace.md).
