OpenPhysics StudioGeometry labEXPERIMENTAL
Open or create a version 1 geometry document.

Experimental: works on version 1 geometry documents through the exact 2D engine, and shows 3d components of boxes and slab layers in a read-only 3D view. The document stays in this page; nothing is stored on the server. It runs no physics.

Parameters

No parameters.

Drag to rotate, Shift-drag to pan, wheel to zoom, click to pick a face. Keys on the view: arrows rotate, Shift+arrows pan, + and − zoom, F fits, 1–6 axis views, 0 isometric.
